# SideQuest listing — English

**Name:** Tremu

**Tagline:** The light gun arcade in your Quest: aim with a real gun or your Touch controllers.

**Description:**

Tremu turns your Meta Quest 3 or 3S into a shooting cabinet. Your games are shown on a large
virtual screen in front of you, and you aim naturally: with the WonderFitter Bluetooth gun (M416,
M17), a USB/Bluetooth gun, or simply the Touch controllers.

- **Arcade and consoles**: arcade (FBNeo, MAME), NES, Super Nintendo, Nintendo 64, Mega Drive,
  Saturn, PlayStation 1 and 2, PSP, Dreamcast, Naomi, GameCube, Wii, Xbox and Xbox 360, together in
  a carousel with posters.
- **PS3, Wii U and PS Vita** through our companion apps, started and left from Tremu.
- **Guns**: WonderFitter without its app, mounting settings per gun, recoil rumble, the consoles'
  GunCon and light guns emulated.
- **Two to four headsets together**, on the same Wi-Fi or over the Internet with a simple code,
  with no paid server.
- **Depth**: stereo pictures in some PS2, N64 and Xbox 360 games; a black background or your real
  room around the screen.
- **Add your games without a cable** from a PC or phone browser.

Tremu is free and open source (GPL v3), its code is public. **No games, BIOS, firmware or keys are
included**: use your own copies. Install guide: https://github.com/proelitem/tremu/blob/main/docs/install/en.md

**Tags:** emulator, arcade, light gun, gun, retro, multiplayer, WonderFitter
